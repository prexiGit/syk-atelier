The journey

From first stitch to certificate

  1. 1

    Step 1: Learn inside the atelier

    Hands-on crochet and handmade-craft training alongside our makers, for a minimum of 120 hours.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Complete your responsibilities

    Each intern's duties and hours are recorded, and they appear on the final certificate.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Receive your certificate

    A certificate with a unique ID and QR code is issued, and your name joins the list below.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Verify it anytime

    Anyone can scan the QR code to open the original certificate on this website.
